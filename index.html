Block Rise ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android developed by MadWare Games ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in Türkiye. This policy explains what information the game and the third-party services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: baranad3m@gmail.com

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game has no account system of its own. We do not sell your personal information for money.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, best score, coins, unlocked themes, daily streak, settings and your advertising consent choice) is stored locally on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

5. Why we process information (legal bases)

  • Personalized ads – only with your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in Options → Personalized Ads.
  • Non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and game analytics – our legitimate interest in offering the game for free, keeping it secure and improving it.
  • In-app purchases – to deliver what you bought (performance of a contract).
  • Leaderboards and cloud saves – to provide features you choose to use by signing in to Play Games.
  • Notifications – only with your permission.

6. Advertising and your choices

The game is free and supported by ads. When you first open the game we ask whether you allow personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Options → Personalized Ads. If you choose not to allow it, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your interests. We pass your choice to our ad partners and to Google Analytics for Firebase (so advertising-related uses of data are turned off), and if you do not allow personalized ads we tell our ad partners that you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Under some US state laws (for example the CCPA/CPRA), showing personalized ads may count as "sharing" personal information; choosing "Don't allow" is how you opt out. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

7. Notifications

With your permission, the game schedules local reminder notifications (for example "Your daily puzzle is ready"). They are created on your device; no information is sent to us. You can turn them off in Options → Notifications or in your Android settings.

8. In-app purchases

Coin packs can be bought with real money. Purchases are optional and processed by Google Play. Coins are used only inside the game. See our Terms of Service for details.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers (Unity and Google) may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They protect such transfers with the safeguards described in their own policies (for example the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses).

11. Data retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Cloud saves are kept in your Google Play Games account; you can delete them from your Google account's Play Games settings. Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 14 months. Data collected by our ad partners is retained according to their policies. To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at baranad3m@gmail.com.

12. Security

Data sent by the game and its third-party services is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Contact us at baranad3m@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the game.
